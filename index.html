MAD SCIENTIST

Description

Fight through a lab full of enemies, collect bombs and potions, and defeat the final boss!

Enemies with a shield can only be killed from behind with the laser, by jumping on their heads or through the bomb.

MAD SCIENTIST

Controls

On mobile: use the on-screen touch buttons (D-Pad, Jump, Shoot, Bomb, HP). The HP button uses a potion to heal – same as the R key.
